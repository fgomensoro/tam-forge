This is the ordered interview-practice authority for Phase 1. Baseline dates and sessions are immutable planning lineage; execution changes only actual fields, status, qualification, evidence, and the exact next action.

## Operating contract

- Work the earliest unmet queue item unless an interview override applies.
- Standard coached cycle: commit independent Attempt A, open a new Codex spoken-coaching task with the role, audience, timebox, and final-answer limit, finish the answer without interruption, receive no more than two high-impact English or content corrections, save Attempt B, and return the feedback/transcript for evidence review.
- Corrections should improve clarity and natural English without interrupting every small mistake.
- Coached rehearsal and the final uninterrupted recording are different artifacts. Neither is claimed until it exists.
- A real interview within 72 hours replaces that day's queue question with company- and role-specific preparation. Record the override in actual fields and resume the earliest unmet item later.
- A real interview during the block counts as interview time but never as live-AI work. Codex may help before and debrief after it.
- An override never changes the baseline date, baseline session, queue ID, segment, or coverage lineage.
- The fixed final mock uses `5 + 45 + 10`, stays sealed, has no coaching or Attempt B, and qualifies only after independent rubric scoring.
- Allowed attempt statuses: `pending`, `attempt_a_committed`, `coaching_complete`, `attempt_b_saved`, `completed`, `overridden`.
- Allowed qualifications: `not_assessed`, `qualifying`, `nonqualifying`, `mixed`.

## Interview priority

[[Roadmap/docs/Interview Queue]] owns the ordered 30-question progression. The first ten are: Tell me about yourself; Current role and scope; Why TAM; Why change now; Strongest relevant achievement; Why this company; Difficult customer; Conflict; Failure and learning; Ambiguity and prioritization.

Every ordinary interview cycle commits Attempt A before coaching. Spoken coaching occurs in a new Codex task with scenario, role, audience, timebox, and final-answer limit. The learner finishes before feedback; Codex gives at most two high-impact corrections, including at most one English/delivery correction. The returned handoff and any separate uninterrupted recording/transcript return to the main study task for analysis. A real interview within 72 hours overrides only actual execution and never rewrites baseline lineage. Live AI is forbidden during a real interview.

The final mock on October 2 uses `5 + 45 + 10`, has no coaching or Attempt B, and qualifies only when sealed and rubric-scored.

## Pipeline operating contract

The target is ten quality applications or substantive recruiter replies per full operating week, beginning Week 2. It is not a quota of two rushed applications every day. One tailored application may consume the complete 30-minute block and counts once. Research and automatic acknowledgements do not count unless research produces the exact assigned legacy artifact. Use [[Roadmap/templates/pipeline_action]] and review conversion weekly.

### Competency scale

- 0: not demonstrated or no practical knowledge
- 1: heavily assisted or basic concepts
- 2: developing performance in a straightforward scenario
- 3: independent performance under ambiguity and generally interview-ready
- 4: strong under pressure and at professional depth

## English evidence

Track all six dimensions separately:

- communication effectiveness;
- fluency;
- accuracy;
- vocabulary;
- pronunciation/intelligibility;
- listening.

Unavailable dimensions are `N/A`. Listening is normally N/A for a monologue. Accent is never scored. Daily coaching gives at most one high-impact English/delivery correction. Written evidence cannot demonstrate spoken fluency, pronunciation, or listening.

## Evidence qualification

- Independent Attempt A may qualify only after rubric scoring and only for dimensions demonstrated.
- Same-question Attempt B is coached improvement evidence and cannot raise the estimated level.
- A new independent scenario is required for transfer.
- A fresh Saturday mock or timed assessment may qualify.
- A real interview may qualify only from post-interview evidence; live AI is forbidden.
- Reading, applications, completion, exposure, and guided practice remain visible but do not change skill level.
- Codex acting only as interviewer during a sealed attempt is not coaching; feedback waits until commitment.
- Self-score and Codex rubric score remain separate. Estimated levels update during weekly review.

## Exit states, closure, and Week 7

- `Not assessed`: no valid independent attempt exists; the criterion cannot close and activates Week 7.
- `Assessed—not demonstrated`: a valid attempt exists below the criterion or target; after a valid Week 7 retest, Phase 1 may close only as **Phase 1 complete with gap**.
- `Demonstrated`: qualifying evidence meets the criterion.
- Variance above 15% triggers reforecasting and makes Week 7 provisional.
- Week 7 becomes active after Week 6 when required coverage is incomplete or an exit criterion needs assessment or remediation. It adds no new material and lowers no target.
- After Week 7, `Not assessed` still blocks closure.

Phase 1 closes only when every required legacy coverage record is complete, all four canonical assessments were attempted under their original constraints, every exit criterion is `Demonstrated` or validly closed as `Assessed—not demonstrated` after Week 7, and the next-phase-priorities gate is complete.

## Preserved practice rules

1. All spoken and written outputs are in English.
2. At least 70% of the time must produce an output: a query, diagram, answer, decision, update, plan, or recording.
3. Attempt SQL and cases before using AI. AI critiques the committed answer; it does not generate the first answer.
4. Do not add courses, videos, or articles beyond this list during Phase 1.
5. Record spoken answers. Listen once, score once, redo once. Do not endlessly polish.
6. Use only fictional, public, or assigned-case material. Never paste real credentials, customer data, confidential payloads, or employer-derived content.
7. Do not move unfinished Saturday work to Sunday. Record the gap and use it to prioritize the following week.

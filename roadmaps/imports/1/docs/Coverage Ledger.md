## Field contract

- Legacy ID: exact unique `stable_id` from YAML, never renamed.
- Required: exact legacy required flag; omitted in YAML means true.
- Source path/heading: exact YAML day values and one of four archived weekly paths.
- Objective/output: preserve exact objective and contract required output; human table text may be concise only because canonical detail comment is exact.
- Original constraint: preserve assessment/no-AI/independence/AI role.
- Status enum: `completed`, `in_progress`, `pending`, `not_assessed`.
- Phase owner: exactly one atomic completion owner token.
- Continuation: `—` or one exact aligned session token, never list/second owner.
- Planned/actual: planned one nonnegative integer; actual nonnegative integer or `unknown`.
- Evidence: Obsidian links.
- Qualification: `qualifying`, `nonqualifying`, `mixed`, `not_assessed`, `not_applicable`.
- Reconciliation: explain split/integration/wording/timing/optional skip without changing coverage.

A continuation aligns presentation or a checkpoint but never creates a second completion claim. Planned minutes are charged only once to the Phase owner shown in the row.

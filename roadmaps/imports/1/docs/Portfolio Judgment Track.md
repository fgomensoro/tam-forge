# Phase 1 Portfolio Judgment Track

Portfolio judgment integrates incident control, account strategy, business judgment, delegation, expectation management, and communication across several customers. It is a derived composite, not a fifteenth competency and not one isolated interview question.

## Decision dimensions

Use every dimension without reducing judgment to a mechanical score:

- Customer and end-user impact.
- Financial, data-integrity, security, compliance, or regulatory risk.
- Severity and blast radius.
- Urgency, time sensitivity, and risk of deterioration.
- Availability and quality of a workaround.
- Launch, contractual, migration, deprecation, or deadline dependencies.
- Strategic account context.
- Internal capacity and the ability to delegate.
- Diagnostic confidence and the facts still missing.

Every output includes an explicit rank, immediate actions, control/ownership for every account, customer communication and cadence, delegation, reassessment triggers, and the evidence that could reverse the order.

## Pass standard

- Make a clear rank rather than calling everything urgent.
- Keep every account controlled through ownership, delegation, and communication.
- Explain the evidence that would reverse the ranking.
- Reprioritize after new facts change impact, risk, workaround, urgency, or diagnostic confidence.
- Score at least 16/20 on the rubric in [[Roadmap/cases/Portfolio Triage Cases]].

## Final gauntlet behavior

The final gauntlet begins with breadth: rank five competing customer situations, assign ownership, delegate evidence gathering, define communication cadence, and state reversal conditions. It then drills into the selected highest-priority customer for discovery, integration design, production failure, escalation, executive communication, and next-quarter account strategy. Technical depth cannot bypass portfolio judgment.

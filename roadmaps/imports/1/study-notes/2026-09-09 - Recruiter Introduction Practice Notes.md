# Recruiter Introduction Practice Notes

## Validated English and structure

- Introduction structure: current role and company, relevant background, customer and technical ownership, and reason for pursuing TAM work.
- “I co-founded DataNest about three years ago.”
- “I translate customer needs into technical requirements. For larger features, I coordinate delivery with our development team.”
- “Fully committed to this role” expresses dedication; “compromised” does not.
- “Explain the transition to them” is the correct construction.
- A passive co-founder can step away from daily operations without claiming to have left the organization entirely.

## Handover framework

Agree on the transition and owner with the co-founders, document account commitments and open issues, let the new owner lead while observing, introduce the owner to customers, and verify the handover before the new role begins. This is a proposed plan, not a completed transition or a promised timeline.

## Card-ready Q/A

### Card 1
Q: What should a short customer backlog update cover?
A: Current status, customer impact, safeguards, current action, what remains uncertain and the next update time.

### Card 2
Q: Does reserved capacity for new orders prove they are unaffected?
A: No. It is a control intended to keep them moving; actual latency and impact require evidence.

### Card 3
Q: How should unknown recovery time be communicated?
A: State that it is not yet confirmed and give a definite next-update time without inventing an ETA.

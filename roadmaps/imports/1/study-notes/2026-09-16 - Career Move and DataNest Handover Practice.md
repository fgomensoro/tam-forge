# Career Move and DataNest Handover

## Practice answer — Why move now?

“I'm proud of what we've built at DataNest, where I own customer relationships and technical delivery for more than 25 accounts. I've learned that I enjoy both solving technical problems and working directly with customers. Now I want to bring that experience to larger customers and more complex integrations, while working with an experienced team I can learn from. I can contribute hands-on experience across discovery, implementation, go-live and ongoing support.”

## Practice answer — What happens to DataNest?

“I've already talked with my co-founders, and they support my decision. Before starting, I would hand over my customers and daily work to the team. I would still own part of DataNest, but I wouldn't manage its daily operations. I would be fully focused on my new role.”

## Practice answer — Would you still handle emergencies?

“No. After the handover, the team would handle customer issues and emergencies. We've already agreed on that, and I would be fully focused on my new role.”
